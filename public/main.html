04 / Support

Questions,
answered.

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days. Delivery time depends on your location and shipping method.
Returns or exchanges are accepted for damaged or incorrect items within 3 days after delivery. Contact PMD before sending an item back.
PMD pieces use an oversized streetwear fit. Check each product's available size options before ordering.
Tracking details are shared after dispatch. Include your order number when contacting support for an update.
You can pay by credit card, bank transfer, USSD, and other payment options available at checkout.

Still need help? Contact the PMD team at pmdwears@gmail.com.
